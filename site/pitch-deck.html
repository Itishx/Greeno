The moat

He shows up at exactly three moments.

A fourth is how this becomes annoying software, so there is no fourth timer anywhere in the app.

Once a day

Morning greet

The notch drops at your own start time. Calendar, the overnight mail that actually needs you, what is due, and only then the thing you said you cared about.

Thirty minute floor

Stuck nudge

He glances at the screen and mostly says nothing. When he does have something, the notch says so and waits for you to open it. Then he stands down.

Your evening

Night debrief

How did it go. You talk for two minutes, the notebook fills in live on screen while you do, he reacts, and the day closes.

Nudges fire on events, never on blind timers. A timer that goes off while you are deep in something is the fastest way to get uninstalled.

How he learns you

Seven minutes of talking. Zero typed forms.

1 The yap Five questions, out loud. A normal day, what keeps slipping, when your head is clear, what you listen to, and what you wish just happened on its own.
2 The notebook It builds itself on the right while you speak. Routines, habits, focus. Three sections, in your words, and you can edit every line of it.
3 The read back He reads it back and you approve it. The target is nine of ten users approving with no edits at all.
Why it matters Day seven beats day one Onboarding does not end at setup complete. It ends the next morning, when the greet references something you told him yesterday. That is the only proof that counts.

Trust

A closed list. Nothing else, ever.

This is everything he can do to a Mac. It is enforced in code rather than asked for in a prompt, and a test fails if anybody adds to it.

focus.on focus.off app.open app.quit url.open music.play music.pause shortcut.run send a message send mail place a call

Nothing on the list can contact a person. He will remind you to text your mum and he will never text her. Chores get automated. Relationships do not. The two in amber ask every time, because closing somebody's unsaved work is not undoable.

Business model

How we make money.

Free
The someone

The character, the morning greet, the night debrief and the notebook. Everything it takes to find out whether he actually shows up.

Paid
$20 a month

Calendar and inbox, the stuck nudge, focus mode, routines, and Hera dictation. Everything that needs your day, not just your attention.

Why free exists It is the acquisition. Nobody pays for a companion before he has proved he shows up, and proving that takes a week of mornings. It is also the training set. Every free conversation is what makes the next morning's greet sharper, so usage on the free tier is the thing that improves the paid one.
